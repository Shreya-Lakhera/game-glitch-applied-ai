# Game Glitch Investigator knowledge

## Number comparison rules
A guess equal to the secret is Correct. A guess greater than the secret is Too High and the next guess should go Lower. A guess less than the secret is Too Low and the next guess should go Higher.

## Common hint bugs
Reversed Higher and Lower messages make a player move away from the answer. A missed equality check reports a winning guess as merely higher or lower.

## Actual game rules
Easy uses 1 through 20, Normal uses 1 through 100, and Hard uses 1 through 50. Invalid guesses do not consume attempts. A round permits 6, 8, or 5 attempts respectively.
